Mystra

Mystra is a creator-to-business platform designed to help content creators connect with brands and businesses for campaign opportunities. Built to remove friction around discovery, contract management, and secure payments, Mystra empowers both creators and businesses to collaborate with confidence.

Role Product Designer
Timeline 2024
Category Creator Economy • Marketplace

Making creator-brand collaborations feel effortless.

Traditional creator outreach is chaotic—lost in DMs, inconsistent pitch decks, and delayed invoicing. We built a unified creator profile that showcases verified audience demographics, past brand campaigns, and transparent rate cards in one cohesive view.

One platform. Two sides of creativity.

A great collaboration tool must serve both creators and brands equally. We designed two intuitive workflows: brands can launch campaigns and invite vetted creators, while creators can browse open briefs, submit proposals, and track campaign milestones directly in-app.

Making collaboration less complicated.

From content review to milestone sign-offs, communication stays organized. Creators upload drafts, brands leave timestamped feedback, and deliverables are approved without lengthy email chains.

From discovery to collaboration.

Mystra's intelligent discovery matches brands with niche creators based on engagement rate, audience demographics, and brand affinity. Detailed analytics remove the guesswork, letting brands run high-ROI campaigns at any scale.
