Sender Logic

Sender Logic is a delivery product designed to give users real-time visibility, fast package tracking, and effortless parcel booking. Through intentional micro-interactions, clear hierarchy, and friction-free flows, the product was designed around clarity and reliability.

Role Product Designer
Timeline 2024
Category Logistics • On-demand Delivery

Small decisions. A more considered experience.

Every interaction in the delivery experience was questioned. Rather than cluttering the interface, critical actions were prioritized based on context. In transit, live route tracking and ETA take precedence, while post-delivery, receipt generation and feedback happen in seconds. Small details like micro-interactions when sliding to confirm ensure high confidence without slowing down the courier.

Functionality shouldn't come at the cost of clarity.

High-frequency interfaces figure prominently into everyday lives. Delivery couriers need lightning-fast entry and verification, without getting lost in complicated menus or unclear inputs. We reduced visual noise and simplified interaction paths to guarantee that tasks are completed accurately and on schedule.

Delivery, made less complicated.

Managing logistics should be effortless. We introduced a flexible booking flow that serves both personal courier requests and merchant bulk distributions, eliminating friction across touchpoints.
