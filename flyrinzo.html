Flyrinzo

Flyrinzo is an AI-powered social media management platform for small businesses and entrepreneurs, automating content creation, scheduling, analytics, and community engagement in one unified, human-centered workspace.

Role Lead Product Designer
Timeline 2024 — Present
Category AI SaaS • Web & Mobile

Social media work gets fragmented.

Small business owners and lean marketing teams juggle multiple disconnected tools for drafting copy, generating visuals, publishing posts, and reviewing performance. We unified the entire lifecycle into an effortless, friction-free dashboard.

AI should reduce effort, not add complexity.

Instead of overwhelming users with prompt engineering, Flyrinzo provides proactive content suggestions, intelligent caption variations, and contextual image generation that fit the brand's tone.

A connected social media workspace.

Cross-channel scheduling and real-time collaboration allow teams to plan weeks of content in advance, review drafts with stakeholders, and publish across platforms seamlessly.

From product workflow to product experience.

We designed both the public conversion experience and the in-app software to reflect the same warm, approachable aesthetic—making AI feel like a trustworthy collaborator rather than an intimidating black box.
