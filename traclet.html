Traclet

Traclet is a modern shortlet marketplace connecting guests, hosts, and verified local experience providers with seamless booking, calendar management, and trust verification.

Role Product Designer
Timeline 2024
Category Proptech • Shortlet Marketplace

The problem was deeper than the UI.

Finding and booking a reliable shortlet often feels fragmented. Guests worry about misrepresented photos and hidden fees, while hosts struggle with high platform charges and booking management. Traclet was built to restore trust, simplify hosting, and introduce seamless service coordination.

From product thinking to interface design.

We streamlined the discovery-to-booking journey. Clean typography, transparent pricing breakdowns, and direct host messaging ensure guests can browse, compare, and book with complete confidence.

Three roles. One connected ecosystem.

A marketplace thrives when every stakeholder has the right tools. We created dedicated, tailored experiences for Guests, Hosts, and verified Service Providers.

More than a place to book a stay.

Traclet goes beyond accommodation by integrating verified local services directly into the reservation flow—from private chefs and airport transit to on-demand cleaning. Hosts manage operations effortlessly, while guests enjoy an elevated, all-inclusive travel experience.
